HARK is open-sourced robot audition software, i.e. sound processing for
robots. It consists of sound source **localization** modules, sound
source **separation** modules and automatic speech **recognition**
modules of separated speech signals that works on any robot with any
microphone configuration.

[HRI-JP Audition for Robots with Kyoto University (HARK)](http://winnie.kuis.kyoto-u.ac.jp/HARK/)
