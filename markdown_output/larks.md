The Fast Template Detector (FTD) library is an implementation of a
template detector based on SSE optimized fast matching of binary data.
An example of such data are Dominant Orientation Templates (see
\'Dominant orientation templates for real-time detection of texture-less
objects\' by Hinterstoisser et al.).
