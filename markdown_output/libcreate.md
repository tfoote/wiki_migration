## About

**libcreate** is a C++ library for interfacing with iRobot Create and
Roomba platforms. For ROS bindings, see
[create_autonomy](create_autonomy).

## Supported Robots

-   Create 1
-   [Create 2](http://store.irobot.com/default/create-programmable-programmable-robot-irobot-create-2/RC65099.html)
-   Roomba 400 series
-   Roomba 500 series
-   Roomba 600 series
-   Roomba 700 series
-   Roomba 800 series
