## Demo

After installing [mrpt_navigation](mrpt_navigation) and [mvsim](mvsim),
run: ```
roslaunch mrpt_reactivenav2d reactive_nav_demo_with_mvsim.launch
```

[Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9iohyHqGc0g)
