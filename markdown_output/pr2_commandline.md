## Useful Commands

-   rosrun pr2_commandline projector_on
-   rosrun pr2_commandline projector_off

(These are particularly useful when an application turns the textured
light projector on and does not turn it off when it exits)

-   rosrun pr2_commandline spine_height 0.0
-   rosrun pr2_commandline spine_height 0.42
-   rosrun pr2_commandline spine_height 1.0

This moves the spine to the desired percentage of its total height.
